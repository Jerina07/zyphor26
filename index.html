Official Guidelines

HACKATHON INSTRUCTIONS

  • Date: 28/08/2026
  • All participants must report to the Grace College Auditorium by 9:00 AM.
  • The hackathon will conclude at 5:00 PM.
  • Per Head Online payment cost ₹250. Offline payment cost ₹300.
  • The last date for registration is 24/08/2026. Please make sure that you complete your registration before the deadline.
  • Each team can have a maximum of 3 participants.
  • A WhatsApp group will be created, and the team leaders can join the group.
  • Each team must prepare a PowerPoint Presentation (PPT) and bring it with them.
  • Each team will be given 5 minutes to present and explain their PPT.
  • Participants must bring their own laptops for the hackathon.
  • Once you have registered, you will be able to select your preferred problem statement one day before the hackathon. Registrations will not be accepted after the deadline.
  • The problem statements will be released one day before the hackathon on 27-08-2026 at 9:00 AM.
  • Participants must develop their website at the hackathon venue based on the selected problem statement.
  • The name you enter in the registration form will be printed on your certificate.
  • Cash prizes will be awarded to the 1st, 2nd, and 3rd place winners.
  • Certificates will be provided to all participants.
  • Lunch will be provided for all participants.
